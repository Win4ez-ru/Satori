# ADR 0052: Goal-local owned news and care realization

- Status: Superseded in part by ADR-0054 only for active affect realization as V34; other decisions remain accepted
- Date: 2026-10-02
- Supersedes: ADR-0051 only for active provider expression as V33. Its closure grammar,
  direct-stop bounds, completeness-prefix correction and mixed style/direct-self scope fixes
  remain accepted. Historical V31/V32 requests and all other boundaries remain unchanged.
- Related: ADR-0045, ADR-0047, ADR-0048, ADR-0049, ADR-0050

Acceptance applies to this scoped implementation design, not to candidate character quality.

## Context

Diagnostic 7 completed the separately authorized V31/V32 comparison on 12 paid calls, with
12 replies, no retries and USD 0.036554 exact usage cost. The first human feedback was frozen
before assignment disclosure: A is V32 and B is V31. The user prefers B in pairs 1, 2, 3, 5
and 6; pair 4 criticizes recap without choosing a variant. B's ordinary greeting and restrained
closure are generally acceptable, while both news and depletion replies still miss the target.
Brief praise followed by a relevant question or useful offer of help could suffice for the news.
The objection is relatively better in B but could express more character. These are relative
preferences, not five accepted replies, a whole-character acceptance or a universal policy ranking.

The narrow request diagnosis
found identical relevant agency, cognition, delivery and native caps in turns 3/4. Both policies'
actual assembled requests contain the anti-recap principle, one peer block and the complete
cognition support boundary. Admissible event/state facts do not require restatement;
`listen_and_reflect` and `presence_before_advice` do not require a recap passage. This evidence
does not demonstrate a selector defect or prove that one instruction caused the sampled output.
Another global prohibition would not explain the existing failure.

After this diagnosis the user explicitly authorized continuing the scoped correction. That
authorization permits implementation and local verification, not a new paid experiment,
character acceptance or another phase/stage. V31 is a delivery reference, not the accepted
character baseline; V10 remains the last accepted baseline.

## Decision

Introduce policy V33 inside Checkpoint 14.3 Phase A. Restore the V31 provider envelope,
behavior principles, existing ordinary-initiative guidance and understated closure as the
realization reference, with V33 version metadata and two goal-local clarifications below.
This replaces the active compact V32 expression. It does not roll back V32's deterministic
closure or mixed-request correctness fixes. Historical V31/V32 branches remain reproducible.

The application still freezes one agency decision before cognition completion. Act, subject,
initiative, lead, authoritative personality/value/affect/relationship projections and cognition
obligations remain application-owned. The provider realizes that decision in one reply. The full
cognition support boundary remains rendered exactly once. Nothing in this decision makes
provider prose authority about self or lets the provider reselect the movement.

### Ordinary news

Apply the clarification only to the already selected ordinary acknowledgement with
`current_exchange` subject and `celebrate_and_continue` delivery goal, with no existing typed
`REQUEST` or `QUESTION` obligation. Act/subject/goal alone do not establish ordinary scope:
news combined with an explicit task can still have that same selected movement. The request
builder passes its existing cognition-derived `answer_required` as a transient `protected_request`
renderer bound; only V33 uses it to exclude the two new clarifications. No schema or selector
change is needed, and historical rendering through V32 ignores this new argument. Supplied
event facts bound what Satori may claim; they are not compulsory reply parts. A brief own positive evaluation or
praise can acknowledge the news without repeating the user's completed work or explaining why
the user should be pleased.

The existing request detector uses a closed cue grammar, not a complete semantic parser. Each
new clause also conditions short-reaction sufficiency on absence of a same-message factual,
explanation or concrete-work request: any such request still requires its complete answer or
work and cannot be replaced by a reaction. This preserves task obligations even where a wording
such as `Поясни отдельно…` does not acquire the existing `REQUEST`/`QUESTION` signal. It is a
provider-facing boundary, not proof that all such requests are detected or fulfilled. No new
intake signal, parser cue, semantic judge or retry is introduced.

If this turn already selects `advance_current`, a grounded detail can support a specific question
**or** relevant useful help. Neither is a mandatory sentence sequence, and a question mark is
not an initiative criterion. An unrelated advice passage is not an adequate conversational hook;
a concrete offer about the actual problem is allowed. Without a grounded hook, a complete short
reaction remains valid. Do not invent missing project details, hobbies, prior exchanges or actions.

This does not create an initiative opportunity, broaden adjacent-interest access, change selection
frequency, require a question on every news turn or reinterpret the qualitative 10–20% preference
as a quota or accepted measurement. Explicit tasks, corrections, safety, distress, listening,
repair and other protected requests retain their existing route and obligations.

### Ordinary care

Apply the clarification only to the already selected ordinary care with `user_explicit_state`
subject and `practical_care` delivery goal, with no existing typed `REQUEST` or `QUESTION`
obligation. Combined depletion/task or direct factual questions retain the unchanged V31 guidance
and complete cognition boundary instead of the new short-reaction permission. The supplied state
establishes the factual boundary, not a duty to introduce care by repeating symptoms or explaining
the user's feelings. Satori's
own direct participation can satisfy conversational presence. A short caring reaction may be
sufficient; an advice passage, a causal explanation, a question and a visible character flourish
are not mandatory.

Existing safety/distress, explicit listen requests, correction, repair, factual and task paths
remain protected. This clarification cannot conceal required help, invent a diagnosis or hidden
cause, deny the user's state, require teasing or simulate canonical offense. It does not change
care selection, affect appraisal, relationship authority or the `stay_on_topic` initiative bound.

### Retained correctness and ownership boundaries

V33 retains the V32-and-later whole-turn closure grammar and typed `CharacterClosureKind`.
`end_discussion` blocks a canonical-interest pivot and broad adjacent-interest read at the existing
closure priority. `claimed_resolution` records wording only; it does not prove that an earlier
question was answered. Bounded recent dialogue remains context, never a Stage 17 pending-thread
owner or new self evidence. A restrained close may preserve an evidenced disagreement, without
requiring another question or demanding concession.

For V33 only, an actual `close` movement with typed `claimed_resolution` also retains the exact
V32 claimed-resolution guidance alongside V31's understated closure. It checks the supplied
dialogue without mechanically treating the formula as an answer, demanding repetition or inventing
disagreement after resolution. Other acts and closure kinds do not receive that clause; V33
rendering requires the current typed evidence. This adds no history-answer detector or persistent
pending-question state.

The anchored unquoted completeness-prefix correction and genuine mixed style/direct-self scope
support remain versioned as in ADR-0051. Existing authoritative self facets and coupled-facet
validation remain binding; style correction stays primary and creates no new self-disclosure
priority. Historical versions through V31 retain their original treatment and explicit failure
controls outside their frozen corpus.

No persistent state, owner, seed, table, migration, model, native generation cap constant,
temperature policy, validator reason or retry ceiling changes. Agency/delivery/presence/manifest
schemas remain 1/5/3/17 and cognition remains 2/3. The V33 expression marker joins the existing
shared max-one retry path with the same selected movement and authoritative evidence. No reply
template, phrase bank, output rewrite, judge model, extra provider call or raw chain-of-thought
storage is introduced. The earlier corrected direct-stop selection still uses the existing
96-token close cap rather than the historical 384-token interest-share cap; retaining that
correction is not a claim of parameter equality against every V31 control.
