# ADR 0047: Familiar peer character delivery

- Date: 2026-09-13
- Supersedes: ADR 0045 only for active provider realization, V29 mixed disclosure routing, correction precedence and closure-cue recognition;
  V28 agency topology, historical policies, runners, samples and reviews remain unchanged
- Related: ADR 0020, ADR 0021, ADR 0023, ADR 0026, ADR 0043, ADR 0045

## Context

Direct human phase 1 found V28 less recognizable than V27: 4/18 versus 9/18, despite
grounding/completeness passing 36/36. The later partial phase-2 feedback cannot be finalized:
three act scores and one cross-session score are unknown, and two attestations were negative.
Do not convert that feedback into a complete formal review. The user qualitatively rejected
both variants, preferred V27's lower rate of restatement, then supplied and endorsed examples
of ordinary direct, playful and occasionally stubborn conversation in good relationships.
On 2026-09-13 the user authorized implementation from those examples within Checkpoint 14.3.

The examples are semantic design references, not canonical self facts, replay transcripts or
desired provider replies. They assume established positive relationships and no current strain;
the old six-session sample began with fresh identities. Familiarity must therefore be tested
as a controlled owner-state contrast, not silently added to production identity or relationship.

Inspection found that V28's final renderer omits the already selected delivery voice, reduces
personality to one posture and verbalizes affect labels. Its relationship relevance excludes
many ordinary social turns. Its anchored closure detector also misses conversational hedges
and punctuation in the exact public final turn. A correct agency label consequently does not
prove recognizable conversation, and a missed closure may select another exploration move.

## Decision

Policy V29 uses one replacement renderer of the existing presence schema 3 / delivery schema 5 /
agency schema 1. It preserves the full cognition boundary, exact disclosure facets, grounding,
provenance, initiative bounds and fallback. It renders the selected voice, current personality
signals (including evolution directions) and relevant relationship/affect as expression guidance.
No second personality store, new state owner, model judge, output rewrite or additional call is
introduced. Persistent values, traits, relationships and memories are unchanged.

Ordinary social/affect/self/achievement/depletion/objection turns may use the canonical
relationship projection for expression. Unknown relationships do not imply closeness or harm;
technical work does not become colder or less complete because of unrelated relationship state.
Established positive relationships can license informal warmth, brief teasing, playful
non-disclosure and caring directness. They do not require a joke, grant permissions, license
humiliation, or override explicit listen requests, distress, correction, repair and safety.

State should shape the reply without becoming a narrated inventory. A social check-in can have
an ordinary short answer and a reciprocal question when no active correction forbids it.
Current-interest playfulness cannot conceal an invented hobby or off-screen activity; a direct
factual identity/capability inquiry still receives the requested truthful answer. Unknown project
details invite an honest question, not a presumed project. A sleep/stress explanation requires
actual supplied evidence. Disagreement may be firm and retain a current reasoned view after a
topic is stopped, but cannot invent a prior position, demand concession, or prolong an explicit
stop. Assistant history is conversational context, never new durable self evidence.

The V29 evidence analyzer recognizes bounded whole-turn colloquial topic endings; only the
listed conversational hedges are allowed. Its grammar excludes quotes, questions, negation,
reported speech and hypotheticals. Older policies retain their exact analyzer behavior.
No test-input-specific replacement, reply template or forced catchphrase is permitted.

The local production regression exposed an older mixed affect/relationship failure: selection
returns the existing relational `owned_response`, while the manifest demands `self_disclose`,
whose closed facet contract excludes relationships. For V29, that exact mixed disclosure class
uses the relational route and retains both mandatory facets. Closed delivery schemas and
historical V28 classification remain unchanged. The final expression marker also joins the
existing retry recognizer so the byte-identical realization stays last inside the same message.
The long coherence corpus exposed a second pre-existing mismatch: a hypothetical correction can
select repair in the kernel while cognition prioritizes a direct self inquiry or uncertainty.
V29 respects those existing intake priorities before choosing repair. The topology and the
cognition-owned hard checks are unchanged; historical selection retains its original behavior.
Mixed affect/relationship attributions without a question mark still receive the existing
trusted-state relational reply route, without changing their cognition signals.



## Verification and acceptance

Use versioned public-input tests for fresh/established/strained/unknown relationships, affect,
ordinary and technical turns, missing memory/interests, explicit corrections, high distress,
repair, truthful disagreement and closure. Freeze pre-change V28 request hashes; historical
policies must reproduce them. Verify full cognition/grounding preservation, same-interaction
max-one retry, replay and no owner mutation. Foundation checks remain mandatory.

Real local CLI/provider runs are technical sampled evidence only; Qwen remains an unaccepted
foreground character model. Report actual responses and numeric timings for public scenarios,
without copying private prompt/context into benchmark logs. A later exact paid experiment needs
its own frozen plan and authorization; consumed V28 claims are never reused. User-authored or
assistant-authored examples do not count as runtime evidence. Human recognition remains the
character criterion; the agent performs technical analysis rather than asking the user to design
internal personality fields. V29 remains unaccepted until sampled human review succeeds.

Phase B, Checkpoint 14.4 and Stage 15 remain unopened. No paid inference is authorized here.
